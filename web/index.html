QUICK START / WEEK 01

From pocket to pipeline.

A small setup for a measurable acquisition workflow.

01

Run your PC collector

Requires Node.js 22 or later. In the downloaded project folder:

npm start

Copy the collector token printed in the terminal. The data stays in the data/ folder on your PC.

02

Give it an HTTPS address

Use an HTTPS tunnel pointing at localhost:8787, for example Cloudflare Tunnel:

cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8787

Restart the collector with your Vercel origin in ALLOWED_ORIGINS. Open Connection settings and enter the tunnel URL and token.

03

Pair the Galaxy A15

Create a session, open its link in Chrome on the phone, and tap Start capture. Allow camera and motion access; GNSS is optional.

Keep the page visible and screen awake. A missing gyroscope is reported as unavailable, never replaced with invented measurements.

04

Record, inspect, replay

The PC saves JPEG frames, per-frame metadata, IMU CSV, telemetry JSONL, clock estimates and a session manifest.

Stop & save freezes the session. Replay follows its original frame timestamps and pairs each frame with nearby sensor data.

Acquisition contract

Transport
HTTPS POST / timestamped JPEG frames + batched telemetry. Bounded queue; overload skips are counted.
Coordinates
Phone portrait +X right, +Y top, +Z out of screen. Vehicle and camera extrinsics are uncalibrated.
Angular velocity
Browser rotation rate converted from deg/s to rad/s: X←beta, Y←gamma, Z←alpha.
Timing
Decimal nanosecond fields encode browser monotonic time. Nanosecond units do not imply nanosecond precision.
Calibration
No intrinsics or distortion calibration is claimed. Native Camera2/SensorManager is the next step for precise hardware timing.
Week 01 gate
Every frame timestamped; real accelerometer + gyro available and alignable; measured 2-minute recording, replay and stress report. Hardware gate remains unverified until tested on your phone.
PC CONNECTION

Connect your collector.

The website runs on Vercel. Your PC receives and stores the data.

On this PC open localhost:8787. Connect the Android phone by USB, enable USB debugging, and approve this PC on the phone.

Platform-Tools (ADB) is required on the PC.

The token is kept in this browser tab's session storage. It is never included in the phone link.
