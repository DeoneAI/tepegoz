# Models, datasets and upstream code

Downloads remain under ignored `.tools/`; phone recordings remain under ignored `data/`. Exact asset URLs, versions, byte counts and SHA-256 values are in [the asset registry](../configs/assets.json). Dataset subset manifests preserve source names and per-file hashes. The local adapters are implementation code, not copies of the training repositories.

SORT, ByteTrack, VINS-Mono, ORB-SLAM3 and preintegration papers are cited in the [methods guide](PERCEPTION-AND-ESTIMATION.md) and [VO/VIO/SLAM study](VO-VIO-SLAM.md). The local tracker is inspired by SORT/ByteTrack; it is not their official implementation. Local attitude fusion is not VINS-Mono or ORB-SLAM3.

Runtime dependencies are listed separately in `scripts/requirements-*.txt`; their own licenses continue to apply. CPU execution was used for measured inference. GPU performance and VRAM were not measured. Preserve model/dataset notices when sharing evidence; do not redistribute phone recordings automatically.
